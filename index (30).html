The stages of an expense

Every expense moves through these stages, worked out automatically from what's filled in:

  1. Confirming WBS/SO: the expense is received (expense date), no PR yet.
  2. Waiting for PR Approval: a PR is entered.
  3. Waiting for Final Invoice/POE: a PO is entered.
  4. Waiting for Remittance Advice: a GR is entered. Sent to AP turns Yes automatically with the GR.
  5. Posted: Posted is Yes, with its Posted Date.
How the SLA is counted

Each stage has an allowed time per country and urgency (see SLA thresholds above). The clock starts from the stage's date (expense date, PR Date, PO Date or GR Date) and counts Monday to Friday only, 24 hours a day, in Philippine time. Weekends are skipped. If the step is logged on the same day as its date, the clock starts from the time it was entered; if it's backdated, from 12:00 AM of that date.

Due within 24h means less than 24 working hours are left. Overdue means the allowed time has passed.

Who gets credit for a step

PR, PO, GR and Posted are credited to the step's Created by. On Auto, that's whoever was logged in when the step was saved. If someone logs a step for a colleague, they pick the colleague's name, and the credit goes to the colleague. Sent to AP follows the GR. Amendments go to Amended by. The expense's creator and the assignee are never used for credit. The History still shows who actually typed it.

How counts and date filters work

Every process is counted on its own date: PR on PR Date, PO on PO Date, GR and Sent to AP on GR Date, Posted on Posted Date, amendments on the amendment date. An expense received in September and posted in October counts as Posted in October.

The Date of dropdown next to the from/to dates chooses which date the filter checks. "Date of any step" keeps an expense if any of its steps happened in those dates.

Dashboard: Team activity and reports

Team activity shows how many PRs, POs, GRs, Sent to AP, Posted and amendments each person did in the chosen period. Click a name to filter, click a number to see the exact expenses, and use Export to Excel for the same data in a file.

Generate report (top right of the Dashboard) makes a PDF or Excel report for the dates and sections you choose, following the dashboard filters. It remembers your last choices.

Expenses table

Newest expense date first. The first 200 matching expenses are shown; use Show more or Show all for the rest. Watching shows only starred expenses, Show archived includes archived ones, and the search box finds archived expenses by ID, PR, PO or GR. CSV export always includes every matching expense.

Notes, amendments and history

Notes form a thread: adding a note never erases older ones. The table shows the latest note; open the expense to see all of them. Every change, old value to new value, is in the expense's History tab and in the Activity Log. The Activity Log loads the newest entries first; use Load older activity at the bottom to go further back.

Notifications and reminders

The bell shows each event once: someone @mentions you, assigns you an expense, an expense of yours is due tomorrow or becomes overdue, someone changes an expense you watch or amends a step you created, and your reminders. The red number counts unread ones only. Mark all as read sets it to 0; after that only new events count.

Watch (the star) follows any expense. Remind me sets a personal reminder; Snooze moves it one day, Done clears it. An unfinished reminder stays in the Reminders tab marked "past due, not done".

Data check, Recycle bin and Archive

Data check lists wrong-year or future dates, dates out of order, missing dates and duplicates. Fix them, or mark them correct if they really are.

Delete moves an expense to the Recycle bin, where it can be restored with its full history, or deleted forever.

Archive moves posted expenses older than the chosen period out of the everyday list to keep the system fast. They still count in the dashboard, can be found by searching their ID, PR, PO or GR (or with "Show archived"), and open read-only. Un-archive to change one.

Your preferences and greeting

The greeting changes by Philippine time: Good morning (12:00 AM to 11:59 AM), Good afternoon (12:00 PM to 5:59 PM), Good evening (6:00 PM to 11:59 PM). In My preferences you can show your own overdue, due-today and on-track counts under it, turn the Monday summary on or off, choose whether watched expenses stop automatically once Posted, and reopen What's new.

Saving status

Next to "Live" at the top: All changes saved, Saving, Offline (changes are kept and save when you're back online, so keep the page open) or not saved (the change failed; make it again).
